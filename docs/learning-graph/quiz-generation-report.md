## Known Limitations

- **Concept coverage is 234 of 532 chapter concepts (44%).** Ten questions per
  chapter cannot reach the 75% target when chapters list 15–35 concepts. The
  questions focus on each chapter's main ideas: concepts named in the title,
  given their own section, or shown in bold.
- **The correct option is the longest one in 121 of 230 questions (53%).**
  Most of these are near ties. Six clear cases were fixed by rewording, but a
  test-wise reader could still find a slight "pick the longest" pattern.
- **Some apply questions use illustrative numbers** worked out with the
  chapter's own method, so they do not repeat the chapter's worked examples.
  These numbers are invented for the exercise and are not facts.
- **Still-developing cases** (South Korea's AI textbooks, the LAUSD/AllHere
  chatbot, Unbound Academy) keep the chapters' "as reported" wording. These
  questions should be rechecked when the chapters are updated.
- **Chapter 16** tests Lewin's and Bridges' models, which the learning graph
  places in Chapter 17. They are tagged with the broader concept
  "Organizational Change."

## Recommendations

1. When chapters are revised, rerun the validator and rebuild
   `quiz-bank.json` so the bank matches the quizzes.
2. Raise coverage in concept-heavy chapters (6, 13, 15 and 19) with a second
   quiz set drawn from `quiz-bank.json`, or with 12–15 questions per quiz.
3. Lengthen some distractors in the near-tie questions to remove the slight
   "pick the longest" cue.
4. Use `quiz-bank.json` for LMS export and for practice questions in the
   chatbot.
