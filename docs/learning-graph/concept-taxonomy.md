# Concept Taxonomy

Each concept in the learning graph belongs to one of 14 categories. The categories follow the order of the book: foundations first, then the evidence, the school day, school culture change, practice, and stories.

| Category | TaxonomyID | Concepts | What belongs here |
|----------|-----------|---------:|-------------------|
| Foundations of Schooling and Learning | FOUND | 62 | Basic ideas about schools, students, teachers, learning, assessment, funding, and evidence, including direct instruction and the industrial-era school day |
| AI Capability Growth | AIGRO | 39 | AI and large language models, the METR task-horizon studies, exponential growth and doubling time, forecasting, AI cost, and AI energy use |
| Five-Level Textbook Model | LEVEL | 34 | Intelligent textbooks, the SAE J3016 analogy, the five levels and their features, level classification, and level-specific policy |
| Content Economics and OER | ECON | 27 | Content creation cost, publishers, open educational resources and licenses, commoditization, the OpenBook QR Score, and cost per student |
| Low-Cost Learning Technology | TECH | 35 | Learning graphs, MicroSims, xAPI, Learning Record Stores, static web hosting, and adapting to students without live AI |
| Privacy and Data Governance | PRIV | 26 | Student data, FERPA, COPPA, GDPR, the level 3 privacy inflection point, data governance, algorithmic bias, and data breaches |
| The Academic Morning | MORN | 36 | Adaptive and mastery learning, AI tutors, guardrails, cognitive offloading, the shorter morning, and critical and computational thinking |
| Social Learning and Human Skills | SOCL | 28 | Constructivism and social constructionism, collaboration, teamwork, communication, creativity, empathy, and social-emotional learning |
| The Pro-Social Afternoon | AFTN | 26 | Project-based learning, the arts, sports, clubs, maker spaces, service learning, and keeping the afternoon structured |
| Technology Adoption | ADOPT | 31 | Diffusion of innovations, adopter categories, innovation attributes, the chasm, and the hype cycle |
| Organizational Change Models | CHNG | 61 | School culture, the grammar of schooling, and the change models of Schein, Lewin, Kotter, ADKAR, Bridges, Senge, Fullan, and CBAM |
| Microstrategies and Stakeholders | MICRO | 32 | Small, concrete change moves, the Switch framework, resistance to change, and engaging teachers, unions, families, students, and boards |
| School Design and Practice | PRAC | 54 | The changing teacher role, the AI-native school day, scheduling, staffing, procurement, pilots, assessment, equity, and making change last |
| Stories and Case Studies | CASE | 41 | Storytelling methods, the risk-and-response framework, and the real cases from the [AI-First Stories](../appendices/ai-first-stories.md) appendix |

No category holds more than 12% of the concepts, well under the 30% limit.
