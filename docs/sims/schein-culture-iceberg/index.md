## About This MicroSim

Edgar Schein describes an organization's culture in three layers, and an iceberg shows the most important thing about them: how much you cannot see. Above the water are a school's **artifacts**, such as its building, schedule, trophy case and pep rallies. At the waterline are its **espoused values**, the framed mission statement and the banner. Below the water is the huge, heavy mass of **basic assumptions**, with beliefs like "a good teacher works alone" and "learning takes a fixed time" frozen into the ice.

The scene shows both sides of culture. The hidden mass is what holds a school steady through hard years. It is also what sinks a reform that steers only at the visible tip, which is the pattern Chapter 18 calls *reform without real change*. The diver with a lamp is the school's response: assess the culture before announcing the change.

## How to Use

- **Explore:** hover over a numbered marker or a label to read about that part of the iceberg. Several callouts include a tip at the bottom.
- **Quiz:** click **Quiz**. The sim names a part of the scene, and you click the marker where it lives. Your score shows next to the buttons.
- **Calibrate (authors):** open [`main.html?edit=true`](./main.html?edit=true) to drag markers, then copy the JSON into `data.json`.

## The 10 Callouts

1. **Cultural artifacts**: the school building with its bell tower, the schedule and the trophy case
2. **School rituals**: the crowd at a pep rally
3. **Espoused values**: the framed mission statement and banner at the waterline
4. **Shared beliefs and norms**: closed doors just below the surface
5. **Basic assumptions (the hidden mass)**: the wide shelf of ice under the water
6. **Assumption: a good teacher works alone**: a single desk in a small, closed room
7. **Assumption: learning takes a fixed time**: a clock fused into the ice
8. **The grammar of schooling**: rows of desks all facing the front
9. **Reform without real change**: the small boat steering at the tip
10. **Culture assessment (the diver)**: a diver shining a lamp on the hidden ice

The iceberg image is a text-free SVG made by `make-iceberg-svg.py` in this folder. The script prints the marker positions it uses, so the callouts stay on their objects if the drawing changes.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will locate Schein's three levels of culture on a picture of a school, give an example of each, and explain why a reform aimed only at artifacts and espoused values can leave practice unchanged.

### Audience

School and district leaders, teachers and instructional coaches.

### Duration

10 minutes.

### Activities

1. **Explore (4 minutes):** hover over all 10 callouts, starting at the top of the iceberg and working down. Note where the stated value and the hidden assumption disagree.
2. **Quiz (3 minutes):** run Quiz mode until you can find every callout without hovering first.
3. **Apply (3 minutes):** list one artifact, one espoused value and one basic assumption at your own school. Which assumption would your next reform run into, and what small, low-risk step could test it?

### Assessment

The learner finds at least 9 of 10 callouts in Quiz mode and can name one assumption in their own school that disagrees with its stated values.
