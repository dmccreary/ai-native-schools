## Notes for the chapter

- Chapter 10 says "The PowerSchool data breach came in December 2024. PowerSchool ... disclosed that attackers had gained access". The intrusion was in late December 2024, but the disclosure came in early January 2025 (S13, S14). Consider "came to light in January 2025".
- The chapter hedges that the missing multi-factor authentication "should be checked against the latest reports". PowerSchool itself told TechCrunch the portal did not support MFA at the time (S13), so the hedge can be dropped.
- The chapter says inBloom's funding came "mainly from the Gates Foundation"; both sources name Gates and the Carnegie Corporation of New York together.
