## About This MicroSim

The Concerns-Based Adoption Model (CBAM) says that people meeting a new practice first have *self concerns*: awareness (stage 0), informational (stage 1), and personal (stage 2). You read six teacher statements about a new textbook and classify each one. The feedback gives a suggested response that answers that kind of concern.

Each stage has two statements. None of the six is resistance, and each is answered by information, not persuasion. The statements are illustrative, written for this chapter.

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stage 0: Awareness | Little concern or involvement; it is not on the person's mind |
| Stage 1: Informational | A wish to know more about what it is and how it works |
| Stage 2: Personal | Concern about what it will mean for oneself |

## How to Use

- Read the statement on the card. The progress strip shows which statement you are on and your running score, "First-try correct".
- Choose one of the three stages with the buttons. If your first answer is wrong, a hint appears and you choose again. Only your first answer counts toward the score.
- **Show meanings** opens a table of the stages. Reading it is exploration, not evidence of mastery.
- **Next** moves on after you read the feedback. On the results screen, **Previous missed** and **Next missed** page through the ones you missed, and **Play again** reshuffles the statements for a practice run.

### Good to Know

- Mastery is 5 of 6 correct on the first try, on the first run. Practice runs give the same feedback but do not change the first-run score.
- The statements are shuffled once per run. Nothing moves until you act, and the MicroSim does not store your answers.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will classify six teacher statements as awareness, informational, or personal concerns, and match each to the response that answers it, correctly classifying at least five of six on the first attempt. (Bloom level: Understand.)

### Audience

Teachers, school administrators, school board members, education policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

- Stages of concern, self concerns, and stages 0 to 2, from the section "CBAM: Concerns at the Start" in Chapter 17.

### Activities

1. **Predict (2 min).** A teacher asks, "Will I be evaluated on this?" Is that resistance? Write your answer.
2. **Classify (5 min).** Work through the six statements. Read the suggested response after each one.
3. **Practice (3 min).** Write one more statement for each stage and swap with a partner.

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim, then check each answer by using it.

1. "What exactly does it do?" Which stage? *(Informational. Respond with a clear description and a demonstration.)*
2. "Will I be evaluated on how quickly I adopt this?" Which stage? *(Personal. Answer directly about evaluation and support.)*
3. Is a personal concern about students? *(No. It is about what the change means for the teacher.)*
4. Does every concern need persuasion? *(No. Self concerns are answered with information and support.)*
