## About This MicroSim

The 21 concepts in Chapter 1 are not a list of separate vocabulary words. They depend on one another. This map shows those dependencies, taken from the book's own learning graph.

Each arrow points **from a concept to a prerequisite it depends on**. For example, the arrow from *Per-Pupil Spending* points to *Public School Funding*: you need to know where school money comes from before you can divide it per student. This direction is the reverse of many diagrams, which draw arrows from a prerequisite to what it enables.

Four concepts are gold: **Education**, **Learning**, **Student**, and **Teacher**. They have no prerequisites in this chapter. Every other concept rests on at least one of them.

The quiz asks six questions. For each, you select every **direct** prerequisite of a highlighted concept: the concepts its own arrows point to, not the ones further down the chain.

## How to Use

- **Explore.** Click any concept to read its definition.
- Press **Start quiz**. The concept for each question has a purple border.
- Click concepts to select them (orange). Click again to unselect. **Clear selection** removes them all.
- Press **Submit**. If the answer is wrong, a hint says whether you missed a prerequisite or picked an extra one, and you get a second try. Only the first try counts toward your score.
- After a correct answer or a second wrong one, the correct prerequisites turn green and the arrows to them are highlighted. Press **Next question**.
- After six questions you see your first-try score and any questions you missed.
- **Zoom in**, **Zoom out**, and **Reset** change the view. You can drag the map to pan. The mouse wheel does not zoom, so the page scrolls normally.

Mastery is 5 of 6 on the first try. The MicroSim does not store your answers.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

After using this MicroSim, the learner can **explain** which earlier concepts a Chapter 1 concept depends on by selecting its direct prerequisites on the concept map, with at least 5 of 6 questions correct. (Bloom level: Understand.)

### Audience

Teachers, administrators, school board members, policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10-15 minutes.

### Prerequisites

- All 21 concepts in [Chapter 1](../../chapters/01-building-blocks-schools-students/index.md), and the meaning of a dependency arrow from the section "Putting the Building Blocks Together."

### Activities

1. **Predict (2 min).** Before you open the quiz, guess how many direct prerequisites *Higher-Order Thinking* has. Many people guess three or more because it sounds advanced.
2. **Explore (4 min).** Click the four gold concepts. Then trace the chain from *Per-Pupil Spending* down to *Education*. How many steps is it?
3. **Quiz (6 min).** Answer the six questions. Follow only the arrows that leave the highlighted concept.
4. **Discuss (3 min).** Why does this book draw arrows toward prerequisites? What would a teacher do with the map when a student struggles with *Learning Gap*?

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim.

1. What are the direct prerequisites of *Direct Instruction*? *(Teacher, Learning, and Classroom.)*
2. *Higher-Order Thinking* sounds advanced. How many direct prerequisites does it have? *(One: Bloom's Taxonomy.)*
3. An arrow goes from *Learning Gap* to *Learning Outcome*. Which one must a learner understand first? *(Learning Outcome. The arrow points to the prerequisite.)*
4. Name the four concepts with no prerequisites. *(Education, Learning, Student, and Teacher.)*
