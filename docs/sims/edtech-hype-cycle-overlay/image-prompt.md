## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, arrows,
callout lines, signs, logos or annotation marks of any kind — including a title or
any heading.** No writing on the certificate, the chalkboard, the computer screen,
the speech bubble or anywhere else. All labeling and the title are added by an
interactive HTML overlay.

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: PNG
- **Dimensions**: exactly 1600 × 800 px (landscape, 2:1)
- **Style**: a painted landscape, in the manner of a warm gouache or soft digital
  painting for an adult education book: clear silhouettes, gentle textures, soft
  light, friendly rounded figures. Every object must still read clearly when the
  whole image is shown about 800 px wide.
- **Light**: dawn on the left (peach sky) turning to clear midday on the right
  (pale blue sky, a soft yellow sun).

## What to Draw

A side view of one continuous grassy landform whose **skyline is the hype-cycle
curve**. Trace a thin golden highlight along the skyline so the curve reads clearly.

- **Far left (0%)**: ground at about 82% from the top.
- **Steep climb** from 10% to 25% from the left.
- **Summit (the peak)**: rounded top at 27% from the left, 20% from the top.
- **Long fall**: down the right side of the peak, through 41% from left at about 54% from top.
- **Valley floor (the trough)**: lowest point at 54% from the left, 77% from the top.
- **Climb out**: a smoother rise from 58% to 82% from the left.
- **Plateau**: flat high ground at about 54% from the top from 82% to the right edge.
- A pale distant hill line behind the landform, and a band of brown soil along the bottom 7% of the image.
