## About This MicroSim

The Concerns-Based Adoption Model describes eight *levels of use*, from 0 Nonuse to VI Renewal. A level describes what a teacher does with an innovation and how fluently, not how often. You read eight behaviors with a new textbook and place each at a level. Each level has exactly one behavior.

Two behaviors both describe daily use, yet sit at different levels: one is mechanical and step by step, the other is smooth and routine. On a wide screen, the results screen lists the levels in order. The behaviors are illustrative, written for this chapter.

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 0 Nonuse | No involvement and no plan to be involved |
| I Orientation | Learning about the innovation and deciding whether to use it |
| II Preparation | Getting ready to use it for the first time |
| III Mechanical use | Using it in a short-term, disjointed way, focused on each step |
| IVA Routine | Using it in a stable way with few changes |
| IVB Refinement | Varying the use to increase its impact on students |
| V Integration | Combining efforts with colleagues for collective impact |
| VI Renewal | Re-evaluating the innovation and seeking major modifications or alternatives |

## How to Use

- Read the behavior on the card. The progress strip shows which behavior you are on and your running score, "First-try correct".
- Choose one of the eight levels with the buttons. If your first answer is wrong, a hint appears and you choose again. Only your first answer counts toward the score.
- **Show descriptions** opens a table of the levels. Reading it is exploration, not evidence of mastery.
- **Next** moves on after you read the feedback. On the results screen, **Previous missed** and **Next missed** page through the ones you missed, and **Play again** reshuffles the behaviors for a practice run.

### Good to Know

- Mastery is 7 of 8 correct on the first try, on the first run. Practice runs give the same feedback but do not change the first-run score.
- The behaviors are shuffled once per run. Nothing moves until you act, and the MicroSim does not store your answers.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will classify eight teacher behaviors by CBAM level of use, correctly placing at least seven of eight on the first attempt. (Bloom level: Understand.)

### Audience

Teachers, school administrators, school board members, education policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10-15 minutes

### Prerequisites

- The eight levels of use, from the section "CBAM, Completed" in Chapter 18.

### Activities

1. **Predict (2 min).** A teacher uses the tool every day. Is she at a high level? Write your answer.
2. **Classify (8 min).** Work through the eight behaviors. Ask what the teacher is doing and how fluently.
3. **Discuss (5 min).** Which support would move a teacher from mechanical use to routine use?

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim, then check each answer by using it.

1. A teacher uses it daily but follows each step with the guide and often loses track. Which level? *(III Mechanical use.)*
2. Three teachers compare dashboards weekly and redesign the morning together. Which level? *(V Integration.)*
3. Are level of use and stage of concern the same? *(No. Level of use describes behavior; stage of concern describes feelings and thoughts.)*
4. Is a higher level always better? *(No. Routine use can be appropriate, and renewal may mean replacing the innovation.)*
