## About This MicroSim

The hype cycle describes how expectations for a new technology rise to a peak, fall into a trough, and then recover as people learn what the technology is good for. You place eight events, one at a time, on one of the five stages. The events come from three stories: the MOOC wave of 2012 to 2013, a large district's 2013 tablet rollout, and the 2022 release of a general-purpose AI chatbot.

Classify by the pattern of expectations an event shows, not by how good the technology is. On a wide screen, the results screen draws every event along the cycle, so you can see the MOOC and tablet stories follow the same shape. The stage assignments are a reading of the evidence, not a measurement.

| Stage | Definition |
|---|---|
| Innovation trigger | A breakthrough or launch sparks interest |
| Peak of inflated expectations | Publicity and expectations exceed what the technology can do |
| Trough of disillusionment | Failures meet the inflated expectations and interest falls |
| Slope of enlightenment | Experiments clarify what the technology is good for |
| Plateau of productivity | The technology is understood and used for what it does well |

- **Next** moves on after you read the feedback. On the results screen, **Previous missed** and **Next missed** page through the ones you missed, and **Play again** reshuffles the events for a practice run.

### Good to Know

- Mastery is 7 of 8 correct on the first try, on the first run. Practice runs give the same feedback but do not change the first-run score.
- The events are shuffled once per run. Nothing moves until you act, and the MicroSim does not store your answers.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will classify eight events in the history of educational technology by the stage of the hype cycle they illustrate, correctly placing at least seven of eight on the first attempt. (Bloom level: Understand.)

### Audience

Teachers, school administrators, school board members, education policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10-15 minutes

### Prerequisites

- The five stages of the hype cycle and EdTech hype waves, from the section "The Hype Cycle and Educational Technology" in Chapter 15.

### Activities

1. **Predict (2 min).** Where do you think AI tutoring is on the cycle today? Write down your guess and one reason.
2. **Classify (8 min).** Place the eight events. Before each choice, ask: are expectations rising, peaking, falling, or settling?
3. **Discuss (5 min).** On the results screen, compare the MOOC and tablet stories. What came after the trough in each case?

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim, then check each answer by using it.

1. A district announces a plan to give every student a tablet. Which stage? *(Peak of inflated expectations.)*
2. Does the trough mean a technology has failed? *(No. The trough is often followed by the slope of enlightenment, where people learn what works.)*
3. Randomized studies show which uses of an AI tutor help and which harm. Which stage? *(Slope of enlightenment.)*
4. Is the peak the moment a technology is most useful? *(No. It is the moment expectations most exceed what the technology can do.)*
