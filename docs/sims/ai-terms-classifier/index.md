## About This MicroSim

The terms *artificial intelligence*, *machine learning*, *generative AI*, *large language model*, and *AI agent* form a ladder: each rung is a narrower kind of the one above it. Every large language model is generative AI, and every generative system is machine learning.

The left side shows six categories as nested regions, with **Not AI** outside them all and **AI agent** innermost. For each of eight example systems you click the **narrowest** region that fits and press **Submit**. Clicking a region also shows its definition.

The examples are illustrative, written for Chapter 4. They are shuffled once per session.

## How to Use

Nothing moves until you act, and the MicroSim does not store your answers.

- **Nested regions:** click a region to select it and read its definition. The innermost region you click is the one selected.
- **Submit:** commits your choice. Two attempts per example; only the first counts.
- **Next:** moves to the next example; after the eighth it shows your score.
- **Try again:** reshuffles the examples and starts over.

Choose the narrowest category whose definition the example meets, not just the one whose topic word sounds right.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

After using this MicroSim, the learner will **classify** eight example systems into the narrowest of six categories (Not AI, AI without machine learning, Machine learning, Generative AI, Large language model, AI agent), correctly placing at least seven of eight on the first attempt. (Bloom level: Understand.)

### Audience

Teachers, administrators, school board members, education policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10 minutes.

### Prerequisites

- The terms artificial intelligence, machine learning, generative AI, large language model, and AI agent, from the section "A Ladder of Terms" in Chapter 4.

### Activities

1. **Read the regions (2 min).** Click each region once and read its definition.
2. **Classify (6 min).** Place all eight examples. Before you submit, say which defining feature decided your choice: learning from data, creating content, using language, or taking actions.
3. **Discuss (3 min).** Why do the spam filter and the course-failure predictor land in the same region? What is the only difference between the chatbot and the quiz-website builder?

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim, then check each answer by using it.

1. A thermostat switches the heat on below 68°F. Which category? *(Not AI. It follows one fixed rule.)*
2. A 1990s chess program uses rules its programmers wrote. Which category? *(AI without machine learning.)*
3. A tool turns text into new spoken audio. Why is it not a large language model? *(It creates audio, not language, so it is Generative AI.)*
4. What makes a system an AI agent rather than a chatbot? *(It takes multi-step actions with tools toward a goal, instead of stopping after it answers.)*
