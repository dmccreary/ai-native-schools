## About This MicroSim

Alpha School and Unbound Academy use the same adaptive software, 2 Hour Learning. They are not the same program. Alpha is an in-person private school that charges about $40,000 (Austin) to about $65,000 (newer campuses) a year, and it has an afternoon of workshops and projects led by adult guides. Unbound is a free online charter school in Arizona, approved by a 4–3 vote, with no in-person afternoon.

The MicroSim shows nine facts, one at a time. You decide whether each is true of Alpha only, Unbound only, or both. At the end, the facts are grouped. The shared software is only one fact; the facts that only Alpha has are the people and the afternoon. The pair shows a benefit and a risk. The software lets students move at their own pace, but Unbound, which has the software without the adults and the afternoon, reported early results below state averages. The book's response is that the software is not the model: a school that copies it must also plan the people and the afternoon. Several details are still unfolding, so check them against the sources.

## How to Use

1. Read the fact in the white box.
2. Click the bin you think fits. The button turns blue.
3. Press **Submit**. The box below the fact says whether you are right and why.
4. If you are wrong, the button you chose turns orange. Choose again and press **Submit**. The second try does not change the score. After a second miss, the right answer turns green.
5. Press **Next** to go on. After the last fact, press **Finish** to see your score, with the facts grouped as Alpha only, Unbound only, and both.
6. Press **Start over** at any time to reshuffle and begin again.

Only your first try on each fact counts toward the score. The facts are shuffled each run. Nothing moves until you act, and the MicroSim does not store your answers.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

After using this MicroSim, the learner can **compare** Alpha School and Unbound Academy by placing nine facts as true of Alpha only, Unbound only, or both, correctly placing at least eight of nine on the first attempt. (Bloom level: Understand.)

### Audience

Teachers, school administrators, school board members, education policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

- Alpha School, Unbound Academy, 2 Hour Learning, Alpha adult guides, and "software is not the model," from the sections "Case 1" and "Case 2" in Chapter 22.

### Activities

1. **Recall (2 min).** List what you remember about each school before you start.
2. **Compare (5 min).** Place all nine facts. Aim for 8 of 9 on the first try.
3. **Explain (4 min).** Using the grouped facts, explain why the same software can lead to different results.

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim, then check each answer by using it.

1. Which school uses the 2 Hour Learning software? *(Both.)*
2. Which school has an in-person afternoon led by adult guides? *(Alpha only.)*
3. Do the two schools differ only in price? *(No. They also differ in delivery (in person or online), the adults, and the afternoon.)*
