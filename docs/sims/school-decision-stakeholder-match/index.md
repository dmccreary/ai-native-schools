## About This MicroSim

Anyone who wants to change how a school uses AI needs to know who can say yes. In a typical U.S. public school district, five groups hold different decisions:

| Stakeholder | Role |
|---|---|
| Teacher | Plans and delivers daily instruction in one classroom |
| Principal | Leads a single school |
| District administrator | Manages district-wide operations, such as the superintendent and department directors |
| School board | The elected body that governs the district |
| Parent and family | The adults responsible for the child at home |

This MicroSim shows ten decisions, one at a time, in a shuffled order. You choose who typically holds each one. The decisions mix the classroom, the school, the district, and the home, so you have to think about authority, not topic.

The pattern is typical, not universal. Rules vary by state and district. The decisions are illustrative, written for this chapter.

## How to Use

- Read the decision on the card, then press the stakeholder you think holds it.
- **Show definitions** opens the one-line description of each role.
- If your first answer is wrong, you get a hint and a second try. Only the first try counts toward your score.
- After the second try, the correct stakeholder and the reason appear. Press **Next** for the next decision.
- After all ten decisions, the results screen shows your first-try score and lets you step through the decisions you missed. **Play again** reshuffles the decisions for practice.

Mastery is 8 of 10 on the first try. Nothing moves until you choose, and the MicroSim does not store your answers.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

After using this MicroSim, the learner can **classify** ten school decisions by the stakeholder who typically holds each one, with at least 8 of 10 correct on the first try. (Bloom level: Understand.)

### Audience

Teachers, administrators, school board members, policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10-15 minutes.

### Prerequisites

- The roles of parent and family, teacher, principal, school administrator, and school board, from [Chapter 2](../../chapters/02-evidence-motivation-stakeholders/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Predict (2 min).** Before you start, guess how many of the ten decisions belong to the school board.
2. **Match (6 min).** Work through all ten decisions.
3. **Map your own district (7 min).** Pick one AI tool your school might adopt. List the decisions it needs (a pilot, a contract, data terms, a family opt-in) and name who holds each one where you work.

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim.

1. Who approves a $900,000 district-wide contract? *(The school board, in a public vote.)*
2. Does the school board set one school's bell schedule? *(No. The principal sets it. The board governs the district; it does not run daily operations.)*
3. Name one decision that belongs to families. *(Whether their child joins an optional program, or what the child reads at home.)*
