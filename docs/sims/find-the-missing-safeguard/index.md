## About This MicroSim

A pilot risk checklist has one line for each of seven risks: screen time, handing thinking over, wrong answers, privacy, cost and equity, fear of job loss, and an unstructured afternoon. Each line names what the pilot must have.

The MicroSim shows six pilot plans, one at a time. Each plan covers six of the seven risks and is silent on one. You find the missing risk. Every plan sounds thorough until you check it line by line, so check **Show checklist** and read the plan against each line. The plans are illustrative.

## How to Use

1. Read the plan in the white box.
2. Click the risk you think fits. The button turns blue.
3. Press **Submit**. The box below the plan says whether you are right and why.
4. If you are wrong, the button you chose turns orange. Choose again and press **Submit**. The second try does not change the score. After a second miss, the right answer turns green.
5. Press **Next** to go on. After the last plan, press **Finish** to see your score, with the missing risk for each plan.
6. Press **Start over** at any time to reshuffle and begin again.
7. Check **Show checklist** to read the checklist. Uncheck it to go back.

Only your first try on each plan counts toward the score. The plans are shuffled each run. Nothing moves until you act, and the MicroSim does not store your answers.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

After using this MicroSim, the learner can **attribute** the weakness of each of six pilot plans to the one risk it fails to address, correctly attributing at least five of six on the first attempt. (Bloom level: Analyze.)

### Audience

Teachers, school administrators, school board members, education policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10-15 minutes

### Prerequisites

- The seven risks and the pilot risk checklist, from the section "A Pilot Risk Checklist" in Chapter 22.

### Activities

1. **Read the checklist (2 min).** Check **Show checklist** and read what each risk requires.
2. **Attribute (7 min).** Find the missing risk in all six plans. Aim for 5 of 6 on the first try.
3. **Fix (5 min).** For one plan, write the line that would close its gap.

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim, then check each answer by using it.

1. A plan has a screen cap, hint-first tutor, log review, loaner devices, a roles statement, and a SAFE-rated afternoon. What is missing? *(Privacy. There is no data agreement or retention schedule.)*
2. Does a plan for the afternoon cover the screen-time risk? *(No. Screen time needs its own cap and breaks.)*
3. Is privacy the only risk that needs a written document? *(No. Fear of job loss also needs a written statement of the teacher's role.)*
