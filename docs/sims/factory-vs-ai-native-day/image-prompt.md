## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, arrows, callout
lines or annotation marks of any kind — including a title, a heading, a sign on either
building, writing on the chalkboards, numerals on the clocks, or words on screens.**
All labeling and the title are added by an interactive HTML overlay. Chalkboards show
only simple chalk shapes. Clock faces show only tick marks and hands.

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: PNG
- **Dimensions**: 1600 × 800 px (landscape, 2:1)
- **Style**: warm, painted editorial illustration with soft brushwork and clean shapes, like a
  high-quality picture-book cross-section ("cutaway") of two buildings. Friendly, simple
  figures with readable poses; no caricature. Suitable for an adult education textbook.
- **View**: straight-on side elevation. Both buildings are cut open with the front wall
  removed, so every room is visible as a box. Floors are flat, horizontal and aligned
  between the two buildings.

## Layout Notes

- Keep the floor slabs, outer walls and the gap between the buildings exactly where the
  percentages say, because the six interactive zones are the six room interiors.
- The two buildings must read as the same three-floor structure, so the comparison is
  floor by floor: same heights, same slab lines, very different use.
- The left building should feel uniform, grey and clock-driven; the right building warm,
  varied and full of movement. Avoid making the left building sinister: it is orderly and
  tired, not cruel.
- Figures must be readable when the image is shown about 800 px wide: clear silhouettes,
  no tiny detail that carries meaning.
- **Again: no text, letters, numbers or symbols anywhere in the image — no signs, no
  writing on boards or screens, no clock numerals, no title.**
