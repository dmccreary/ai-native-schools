## About This MicroSim

When a student team stalls, it is tempting to blame effort or the quiet member. Usually the cause is structural. This MicroSim gives you six vignettes and five causes:

| Cause | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Unclear shared goal | Members are working toward different or vague outcomes |
| Unclear roles | No one knows who is responsible for what |
| No individual accountability | One or more members can avoid doing their part without anyone noticing |
| Poor listening | Members talk past each other or do not take in what others say |
| Unresolved conflict | A disagreement is being avoided or has become personal |

Similar symptoms can have different causes. A silent team may have unclear roles, or it may be avoiding a conflict. Look for the detail that tells them apart. The vignettes are illustrative examples written for this chapter.

## How to Use

Read the vignette, then choose the main cause. Press **Show definitions** to see what each cause looks like. A wrong first answer gives a hint and lets you choose again, but only the first answer counts toward **First-try correct**. After six vignettes, the results screen shows your score and lets you review the vignettes you missed.

### Controls

- **Choice buttons:** commit an answer. A wrong first answer gives a hint and lets you choose again; only the first answer counts.
- **Show definitions:** opens or closes the reference table. Reading it is exploration, not evidence.
- **Next:** moves to the next item after feedback. On the results screen, Previous missed and Next missed step through what you missed.
- **Play again:** starts a practice run with the items reshuffled. Practice runs do not change the first-run score.

Nothing moves until you act, and the MicroSim does not store your answers.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

After using this MicroSim, the learner can **attribute** the main cause of a team's difficulty in each of six short vignettes to one of five causes, correctly attributing at least five of six on the first attempt. (Bloom level: Analyze.)

### Audience

Teachers, administrators, school board members, policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10 minutes

### Prerequisites

- Shared goals, team roles, individual accountability, active listening, and conflict resolution, from Chapter 13

### Activities

1. **Attribute (5 min).** Work through the six vignettes. Underline the detail that points to the cause before you choose.
2. **Compare (2 min).** Two vignettes share a cause but look different. Which are they?
3. **Fix (4 min).** For each cause, name one thing a teacher could set up before the project starts to prevent it.

### Assessment

Mastery is 5 of 6 correct on the first try. Only the first answer to each vignette on the first run counts. Then ask learners to answer these without the MicroSim:

1. Everyone works hard, but on different versions of the project. Main cause? *(Unclear shared goal.)*
2. Is every team problem a conflict? *(No. Unclear goals, unclear roles, and missing accountability look like conflict but are not.)*
3. One student does all the work and the grade cannot show it. Main cause? *(No individual accountability.)*
