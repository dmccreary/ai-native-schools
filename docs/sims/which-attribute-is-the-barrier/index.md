## About This MicroSim

Everett Rogers found that five perceived attributes of an innovation shape how fast it spreads: relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability, and observability. You read eight teacher comments about a new tool and name the attribute that is the barrier behind each one.

Some comments sound alike but differ in cause. "It doesn't fit my pacing guide" is a fit problem (compatibility); "I couldn't get the students logged in" is a difficulty problem (complexity). None of the comments is a matter of willingness. The comments are illustrative, written for this chapter.

| Attribute | Definition |
|---|---|
| Relative advantage | Perceived as better than what it replaces |
| Compatibility | Consistent with existing values, practices, and needs |
| Complexity | Perceived as difficult to understand and use |
| Trialability | Can be tried on a limited basis |
| Observability | Results are visible to others |

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will attribute each of eight teacher comments to the innovation attribute that is the barrier, correctly attributing at least seven of eight on the first attempt. (Bloom level: Analyze.)

### Audience

Teachers, school administrators, school board members, education policymakers, and parents.

### Duration

10-15 minutes

### Prerequisites

- Relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability, and observability, from the section "The Five Innovation Attributes" in Chapter 15.

### Activities

1. **Predict (2 min).** When teachers do not adopt a tool, what reason do leaders usually give? Write it down.
2. **Attribute (8 min).** Work through the eight comments. Ask what the reason behind each comment is before you choose.
3. **Discuss (5 min).** For two comments, name a response that removes the barrier without more training.

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim, then check each answer by using it.

1. "It would mean giving up the group discussions I believe in." Which attribute? *(Compatibility: it conflicts with a valued practice.)*
2. "The contract is for three years with no exit." Which attribute? *(Trialability: it cannot be tried on a limited basis.)*
3. Are complexity and compatibility the same? *(No. Complexity is about difficulty; compatibility is about fit with values, practices, and needs.)*
4. Is every adoption problem a training problem? *(No. Only some barriers, such as complexity, respond to training.)*
