verified-infographic guide. Checked November 2026 with web search and page
fetches. Notes paraphrase the source; they are not quotations.

plus one independent account), **verified, one source** (a single credible
source; no second found), **downgraded** (kept only in softer words),
**removed** (left off the poster).
