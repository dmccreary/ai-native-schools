## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text, letters, numbers, digits, logos,
signs with writing, labels, arrows used as annotations, callout lines or
annotation marks of any kind — including a title or any heading.** All labels and
the title are added by an interactive HTML overlay from a separate data file.
Chalkboards, screens, papers, price tags and protest signs must be blank or show
only simple shapes. Clocks have tick marks only, no numerals.

**No real people, names, school logos, crests, brand marks or recognizable
buildings.** Every person is a generic, invented figure. Do not draw any real
company's software interface.

## What to Draw

### 1. Small private school (column 1, 1–20% from left)

**Scene**: a cozy, sunny room with a cream wall and warm wood floor. A window at the
upper left of the panel (about 3–8% from left, 5–14% from top) shows a sun and a
green hill. Children's paintings hang on the wall to the right. Three children sit
side by side at one wooden table (about 4–14% from left, 18–27% from top), each
with a laptop. An adult guide in a green shirt stands just right of them (about
15–17% from left), leaning in to help. A potted plant sits at the right edge.
**Benefit**: a clock face with tick marks only, a small pale-green wedge shaded at
the top (a short part of the day), with a paintbrush and an orange ball beside it.
**Risk**: tall stacks of gold coins beside a closed iron gate with a padlock; a
small child stands outside the gate.
**Response**: **EMPTY.** Draw the band with a dashed blue border and a very pale
fill, and nothing inside it. This is deliberate and must not be filled.

### 2. Online school at home (column 2, 20.75–39.75% from left)

**Scene**: a quiet living room with a pale blue wall, a window at the upper left
showing a neighbor's red-roofed house, a floor lamp and a small bookshelf at the
right. One child sits alone, seen from behind (about 29% from left, 25–31% from
top), facing a large monitor (about 25–34% from left, 13–23% from top) that shows a
grid of six small classmate faces in a video call. A round rug on the floor.
**Benefit**: a small house with an open door, beside a gold coin inside a green
circle with a diagonal slash (no cost).
**Risk**: a simple bar chart with two short crimson bars that stop far below a
high dashed green target line; a lone child at a laptop sits to the right.
**Response**: **EMPTY**, exactly like column 1: dashed blue border, pale fill,

### 3. High-school math class, two groups (column 3, 40.5–59.5% from left)

**Scene**: a classroom with a cream wall and tiled floor. A green chalkboard at the
top center (about 44.5–55.5% from left, 3–11% from top) shows only a triangle, a

hint).
**Benefit**: a worksheet covered in green check marks, with a thick green arrow
pointing up.
**Risk**: a gray crutch leaning beside a blank exam sheet, with a thick crimson
arrow pointing down.
**Response**: a gray road guardrail running across the band with a glowing yellow

### 4. University lecture hall (column 4, 60.25–79.25% from left)

**Scene**: a wood-paneled lecture hall. A large screen at the top center (about
64–75% from left, 3–14% from top) shows a simple teal staircase and a speech bubble

about 18%, 24% and 31% from top), with 4 to 5 students per tier, each at a laptop.
**Benefit**: a stopwatch with a small shaded wedge, beside a short gray bar and a
much taller green bar.
**Risk**: one small red-brick college hall with a little bell tower, standing
alone on a tiny pink island; around it, faint dashed outlines of several other

**Response**: a blue blueprint sheet with a white staircase drawn on its grid and
a yellow guide rail with dots climbing beside the steps.

### 5. Large public-school classroom (column 5, 80–99% from left)

**Scene**: a bright, crowded classroom with fluorescent lights, a blank white
whiteboard across the top left, and a wall clock with no numerals at the upper
right. Three rows of students (6, 5 and 4 across, front rows larger) all work on
laptops. A mentor in a violet shirt carrying a clipboard walks up the right aisle
(about 97% from left).
**Benefit**: a winding pale-green path of dark green stepping stones leading to a
small gold flag, with two small students at different points on the path.
**Risk**: a wall of six glowing screens beside an open crimson door; two students
are walking out through it, one holding up a blank white sign.
**Response**: a gray laptop being shut, its cord unplugged from a wall socket,
and a raised hand beside it.
