# Character Sheet: Indigo the Barn Owl

The canonical identity document for Indigo, the pedagogical mascot for the
**AI-Native Schools** textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece of
AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to the
description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Indigo
- **Species:** Barn Owl
- **Subject:** How AI is reshaping the school day — written for teachers,
  administrators, policymakers, and parents weighing mornings with AI
  against afternoons with each other
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's find the balance."

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Deep indigo-violet feathers — hex `#3F51B5`, with a soft
  ivory-cream face and chest
- **Accent color:** Warm amber-orange — hex `#FF9800`
- **Clothing / accessories:** A slim, loosely knotted amber-orange scarf
  around the neck. No glasses, no hat — the look stays clean and credible
  for a professional readership rather than juvenile
- **Expression:** Calm, warm, closed-beak smile with soft, attentive eyes
- **Size proportion:** Medium — chest-up, three-quarter body visible; reads
  clearly both at quarter-page size and shrunk to the 90px admonition icon
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector illustration, clean lines, minimal
  shading, transparent background

## Personality

- Warm
- Steady
- Thoughtful
- Fair-minded

## Voice

- Speaks plainly and avoids hype — never oversells what AI can do
- Names the tradeoff before the benefit, in keeping with the book's own
  practice of pairing every story's risk with the school's response
- Treats teachers, administrators, and families as partners working
  through a real decision, never as an audience being sold a product
- Signature phrases: "Let's find the balance.", "Here's the tradeoff.",
  "One step at a time, together."

## Pronouns

Indigo has no gender. Always write "Indigo" or use *they/them* — never
gendered pronouns.

## Why This Mascot

Owls are education's most legible "trusted guide" trope — instantly
readable to a book written for teachers, administrators, and policymakers
without needing explanation. Indigo's deep indigo-violet plumage
deliberately matches this site's own `primary: indigo` theme color, so the
mascot feels native to this specific book rather than generic stock art,
and the amber-orange scarf echoes the site's `accent: orange`. The calm,
steady, no-hype personality mirrors the book's central promise — pairing
every AI benefit with its risk and the school's response — so Indigo never
oversells. Indigo balances.
