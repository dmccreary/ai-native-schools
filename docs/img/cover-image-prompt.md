# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**

- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

*AI-Native Schools* is a book about how a school day could change once
every student has a free, adaptive, interactive textbook. Students would
spend a short academic morning learning at their own pace with AI-built
textbooks, and the afternoon working with each other on music, theater,
sports, robotics, and hands-on projects. The readers are K-12 teachers,
principals, school board members, policymakers, and parents. The tone
should be **hopeful, warm, and grounded**: a credible picture of a better
school day, not science fiction and not a technology advertisement. People
and relationships matter more in this image than machines.

## Title

Place the title **AI-Native Schools** in the center of the image, in a
clean, bold, highly legible sans-serif font, in white or warm ivory with a
subtle drop shadow. Directly beneath it, in a smaller, lighter weight of
the same font, place the subtitle **Mornings with AI, Afternoons with Each
Other**. Spell both exactly as written. Keep a soft, low-detail area of
color directly behind the title and subtitle so they stay readable against
the montage. Do not shrink the title to fit; simplify the background behind
it instead.

## Background Montage

The composition tells one story from left to right: **morning on the left,
afternoon on the right.** The left side has cool, early-morning light in
indigo and soft blue. The right side has warm, golden afternoon light in
amber and orange. The two halves blend smoothly behind the title, like the
sun moving across one school day.

**Left side: the academic morning (calm, focused, one student at a time)**

1. **Self-paced learning** — a student sitting comfortably by a sunlit
   classroom window, reading an interactive textbook on a laptop, focused
   and relaxed.
2. **Learning graph** — a glowing network of connected circles (nodes and
   arrows) floating near the student, a few nodes lit up as "mastered,"
   suggesting a personal learning path.
3. **MicroSim** — a small browser window showing a simple interactive
   simulation with a slider and a bouncing-ball or wave graph.
4. **Exponential growth** — a clean rising curve on a chart that bends
   sharply upward, hinting at fast-growing AI capability (no axis labels
   or numbers needed).
5. **Morning clock** — a simple wall clock showing about 9 o'clock, with
   a soft sunrise glow in the window behind it.

**Right side: the pro-social afternoon (lively, together, hands-on)**

6. **Youth orchestra** — a small group of students playing violins and
   cellos together.
7. **Robotics club** — two or three students leaning over a table,
   building a small wheeled robot together.
8. **Theater and dance** — students rehearsing on a simple stage, one
   mid-gesture, others watching and laughing.
9. **Maker space and project work** — students around a workbench with a
   cardboard model or garden planter, sharing tools.
10. **Team sports** — students in a casual outdoor game on a field, passing
    a ball, in the golden late-afternoon light.

Show a mix of ages from about 10 to 17 and a diverse group of students.
Render every montage element in the same illustration style so the image
reads as one scene, not a collage.

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, **Indigo the Barn Owl**, in the lower-left corner,
sized at about one-fifth of the image height, so it does not overlap the
title. Indigo is drawn in modern flat vector style, chest-up and
three-quarter view, with **deep indigo-violet feathers (#3F51B5)**, a soft
ivory-cream heart-shaped face and chest, calm attentive dark eyes, and a
slim, loosely knotted **amber-orange scarf (#FF9800)** around the neck. No
glasses and no hat. Indigo has a warm, closed-beak smile and one wing
raised in a friendly welcome, looking toward the title. If a reference
image can be attached, use `docs/img/mascot/welcome.png`.

## Style & Composition

- **Illustration style:** modern flat vector illustration with clean lines,
  soft gradients, and minimal shading, matching the mascot's style. Use it
  for every element.
- **Color palette:** deep indigo (#3F51B5) and soft sky blue on the
  morning side; warm amber (#FF9800) and golden orange on the afternoon
  side; ivory and cream highlights throughout.
- **Lighting and mood:** a gentle sunrise glow on the left, warm golden-hour
  light on the right; bright, optimistic, and calm.
- **Composition:** title and subtitle centered; morning scenes in a loose
  arc on the left, afternoon scenes in a loose arc on the right; Indigo in
  the lower-left; generous empty space directly behind the title.
- **Faces:** stylized, friendly, and simple, in flat vector style, so the
  people read as students in general rather than specific individuals.

## Avoid

- Any text other than the title and subtitle. No captions, labels, chart
  numbers, or paragraphs of illegible text.
- Humanoid robot teachers, glowing robot heads, "AI brain" circuitry, and
  other science-fiction clichés. The AI should appear only through the
  laptop, learning graph, MicroSim, and growth curve.
- Rows of desks facing a lecturing teacher, students alone staring at
  screens on the afternoon side, or anything that suggests screens replace
  people.
- Generic stock imagery: handshakes, lone lightbulbs, people pointing at
  whiteboards, graduation caps.
- Photorealistic human faces.
- Montage elements that overlap or compete with the title.
- Any extra mascot accessories (glasses, hat, book, graduation cap).
