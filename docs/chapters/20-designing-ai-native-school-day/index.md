# Designing the AI-Native School Day

## Summary

This chapter covers redesigning the school day itself: the morning-afternoon model, scheduling and staffing, the changing teacher role, and grade-level design choices for a real AI-native school. Readers finish able to draft a daily schedule that pairs a morning adaptive-learning block with afternoon pro-social activities.
