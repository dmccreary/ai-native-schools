# References: AI-First Case Studies II: Lessons for Practice

1. [Altitude Learning (formerly AltSchool)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altitude_Learning) - Wikipedia - The history of AltSchool, the venture-funded personalized-learning school network that closed its schools, rebranded as a software company in 2019, and ceased operations in 2025. A key case of technology arriving before a lasting school culture.

2. [Phenomenon-Based Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenomenon-based_learning) - Wikipedia - Explains the approach in which students study a real topic across subjects, and Finland's 2016 reform that required it alongside subject teaching. Background for the chapter's positive case of Finland's phenomenon learning.

3. [El Sistema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Sistema) - Wikipedia - Describes Venezuela's publicly funded youth orchestra program, founded in 1975, that uses group music-making to build discipline and belonging. The article also notes criticism of its social claims, which makes it a useful two-sided example.

4. Tinkering Toward Utopia: A Century of Public School Reform - David Tyack and Larry Cuban - Harvard University Press - Tyack and Cuban are credited with the idea of the "grammar of schooling," the lasting structures that absorb reforms. Their work explains why so many of the failures in this chapter changed tools but not teaching.

5. Teaching Machines: The History of Personalized Learning - Audrey Watters - MIT Press - Watters is credited with tracing today's personalized-learning promises back to the teaching machines of the 1920s to 1960s. Her history helps readers question result claims that sound new but repeat old hopes.

6. [Feds Charge Once-Lauded AllHere AI Founder in $10M Scheme to Defraud Investors](https://www.laschoolreport.com/feds-charge-once-lauded-allhere-ai-founder-in-10m-scheme-to-defraud-investors/) - LA School Report (The 74) - November 2024 report on fraud charges against the founder of AllHere, maker of LAUSD's "Ed" chatbot, after the company collapsed. A clear case for vendor viability checks before buying.

7. [PowerSchool Begins Notifying Students and Teachers After Massive Data Breach](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/28/powerschool-begins-notifying-students-and-teachers-after-massive-data-breach/) - TechCrunch - Covers the December 2024 breach of a major student information system, entered through a support account without multi-factor authentication. It shows the risk of one vendor holding records for millions of students.

8. [From Chalkboards to Chatbots: Transforming Learning in Nigeria](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/education/From-chalkboards-to-chatbots-Transforming-learning-in-Nigeria) - World Bank Blogs - Results of the six-week 2024 after-school AI tutoring pilot in Edo State, with teachers present. A randomized evaluation found large English gains at low cost; the authors list open questions about long-term effects.

9. [Tutor CoPilot: A Human-AI Approach for Scaling Real-Time Expertise](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03017) - arXiv (Wang, Ribeiro, Robinson, Loeb and Demszky, Stanford) - A randomized trial with 900 tutors and 1,800 students in which AI suggestions helped human tutors, especially weaker ones, at about $20 per tutor per year. Tutors also reported some unsuitable suggestions.

10. [Your Brain on ChatGPT](https://www.media.mit.edu/projects/your-brain-on-chatgpt/overview/) - MIT Media Lab - The project page for the 2025 study finding lower brain engagement and weaker recall in students who wrote essays with ChatGPT. It lists its own limits: a small sample, one task, and a preprint not yet peer-reviewed.
