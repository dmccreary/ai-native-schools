# References: AI-First Case Studies I: Promise and Peril

1. [Alpha School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_School) - Wikipedia - An overview of the private school network founded in Austin in 2014 that uses "2 Hour Learning" software with adult guides. It notes that the school's result claims rely on internal analyses that have not been independently verified.

2. [Intelligent Tutoring System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligent_tutoring_system) - Wikipedia - Explains computer systems that give learners tailored instruction and feedback, and the long research history behind them. Background for judging the AI tutors in the Turkish and Harvard studies.

3. [Case Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_study) - Wikipedia - Describes the case study as a detailed look at one real organization or event in its setting, with its strengths and limits. It helps readers see what a single school's story can and cannot prove.

4. Failure to Disrupt: Why Technology Alone Can't Transform Education - Justin Reich - Harvard University Press - Reich is credited with showing that new learning technologies tend to be absorbed into existing school routines and to benefit already-advantaged students. His argument is a lens for reading every case in this chapter.

5. Oversold and Underused: Computers in the Classroom - Larry Cuban - Harvard University Press - Cuban is credited with documenting the gap between heavy technology spending and modest change in classroom teaching. His work explains why software alone is not the model and why the adults matter.

6. [What Public Schools and Parents Can Learn from a $40,000-a-Year Private School](https://www.the74million.org/article/what-public-schools-and-parents-can-learn-from-a-40000-a-year-private-school/) - The 74 - A January 2026 opinion piece on Alpha School's reported growth results and mastery approach. It also lists concerns: high tuition, an advantaged student body, small samples and unlicensed guides. The author discloses an NWEA advisory role.

7. [Inside the $65K "AI-Powered" Private School Coming to Massachusetts](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/alpha-school-ai-instructors-greater-boston-education/4012295/) - NBC Boston - September 2026 coverage of Alpha's new Greater Boston campuses, where tuition is higher than the original Austin campus. Justin Reich warns that students cannot learn to deliberate with neighbors while alone at a computer.

8. [Tucson Families Join Free AI-Powered Online School](https://www.kold.com/2026/08/07/tucson-families-join-free-ai-powered-online-school/) - KOLD 13 News - August 2026 report on Unbound Academy, Arizona's online charter that uses a two-hour AI academic block. It notes that several other states rejected the school, and that its learning-speed claims are the school's own.

9. [Without Guardrails, Generative AI Can Harm Education](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/without-guardrails-generative-ai-can-harm-education/) - Knowledge at Wharton - A summary of the Turkish high school math study of about 1,000 students. Unrestricted ChatGPT helped practice but hurt exam scores, while a tutor with guardrails avoided the harm, showing design matters more than raw AI power.

10. [An AI Tutor Helped Harvard Students Learn More Physics in Less Time](https://www.kqed.org/mindshift/64694/an-ai-tutor-helped-harvard-students-learn-more-physics-in-less-time) - KQED MindShift (from The Hechinger Report) - Jill Barshay's account of the Harvard physics AI tutor study, with its caveats: fewer than 200 Harvard students, a carefully designed tutor, and no claim that AI should replace instructors.
