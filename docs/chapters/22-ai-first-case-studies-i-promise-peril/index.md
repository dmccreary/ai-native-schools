# AI-First Case Studies I: Promise and Peril

## Summary

This chapter presents a first set of real cases that show both the promise and the peril of AI-first schools: Alpha School, the Turkish high school chatbot study, the Harvard physics AI tutor, and the risk-and-response framework used to weigh them. Readers finish able to analyze a story about AI in schools to find which benefit and which risk it shows.
