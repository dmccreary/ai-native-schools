# Sustaining Change: Systems Thinking and National Adoption

## Summary

This chapter completes the change models with their later, sustaining stages: Senge's systems thinking and mental models, the later stages of ADKAR and CBAM, Bridges' new beginnings, the "grammar of schooling," and Estonia's national AI Leap as a whole-system example. Readers finish able to choose a change model suited to a school's size, culture, and history, and to read a simple causal loop diagram.
