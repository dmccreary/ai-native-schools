# Microstrategies for Leading Change

## Summary

This chapter covers the concrete, next-week moves that make change stick: teacher champions, pilot classrooms, bright spots, shrinking the first step, professional learning communities, and engaging stakeholders — including resistance, family communication, and the changing role of the teacher from lecturer to coach and mentor. Readers finish able to choose a microstrategy suited to a specific teacher's stage of concern.
