# References: Microstrategies for Leading Change

1. [Positive Deviance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_deviance) - Wikipedia - Explains the strength-based approach of finding people who already solve a shared problem better than their peers with the same resources. It is the research idea behind this chapter's "bright spots" and teacher-champion microstrategies.

2. [Professional Learning Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_learning_community) - Wikipedia - Describes how teams of teachers learn together from their own daily practice. Useful background for the chapter's coaching, peer observation, and learning-community moves that help a staff build AI literacy together.

3. [Change Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change_management) - Wikipedia - Surveys the discipline of preparing and supporting people through organizational change, including common models and the reasons change efforts fail. Gives context for treating resistance as information rather than as an obstacle.

4. Switch: How to Change Things When Change Is Hard - Chip Heath and Dan Heath - Broadway Books - The Heaths are credited with the Switch framework: direct the rider (clear steps), motivate the elephant (feelings), and shape the path (the environment). The chapter's bright spots and shrinking the first step come straight from this book.

5. Instructional Coaching: A Partnership Approach to Improving Instruction - Jim Knight - Corwin Press - Knight is credited with the partnership model of coaching, in which the coach treats teachers as equal professionals who make their own choices. It explains why coaching, not mandates, moves hesitant teachers toward new classroom practice.

6. [AI Guidance for Schools Toolkit](https://www.teachai.org/toolkit) - TeachAI - A free toolkit from Code.org, ETS, ISTE, Khan Academy and the World Economic Forum with sample policies, a staff and family presentation deck, and engagement advice. Practical material for the stakeholder and family-communication moves in this chapter.

7. [What Is a "Professional Learning Community"?](https://www.ascd.org/el/articles/what-is-a-professional-learning-community) - ASCD (Richard DuFour) - DuFour's widely cited article sets out three ideas: focus on learning, work in teams, and hold yourselves accountable for results. It shows what a real learning community looks like, as opposed to a meeting with a new name.

8. [The Effect of Teacher Coaching on Instruction and Achievement: A Meta-Analysis](https://annenberg.brown.edu/publications/effect-teacher-coaching-instruction-and-achievement-meta-analysis-causal-evidence) - Annenberg Institute, Brown University - Kraft, Blazar and Hogan pool 60 causal studies and find clear effects of coaching on teaching and smaller effects on achievement. They warn that effects shrink as programs grow, a caution for scaling coaching.

9. [AFT to Launch National Academy for AI Instruction](https://www.aft.org/node/101397) - American Federation of Teachers - The July 2025 announcement of a union-led AI training academy funded by Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic. It shows a teacher union shaping AI training, which matters for the chapter's union-engagement and job-fear discussion.

10. [Positive Deviance Collaborative](https://positivedeviance.org/) - Positive Deviance Collaborative - The home of the positive deviance approach, with guides, tools and case studies, including education projects. Helpful for leaders who want a structured way to find and spread the practices of a school's own bright spots.
