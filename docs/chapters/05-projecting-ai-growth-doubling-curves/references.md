# References: Projecting AI Growth: Doubling Curves, Limits, and Uncertainty

1. [Logarithmic scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logarithmic_scale) - Wikipedia - Explains how log-scale axes space values by multiples rather than equal steps, and why exponential trends appear as straight lines. The core skill readers need to read the METR chart in this chapter.

2. [Exponential growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_growth) - Wikipedia - Defines exponential growth, doubling time, and how it differs from linear growth, with classic examples. Helps readers see why a steady doubling trend produces surprisingly large numbers over a three-to-four-year planning window.

3. [Hallucination (artificial intelligence)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucination_(artificial_intelligence)) - Wikipedia - Describes how AI systems produce confident but false statements, with causes and examples. Supports the chapter's case that AI literacy matters for students and staff even as AI capability grows.

4. Superforecasting: The Art and Science of Prediction (2015) - Philip E. Tetlock and Dan Gardner - Crown - Tetlock showed that careful forecasters beat experts by using base rates, probabilities, and frequent updating. His approach shows how school leaders can plan around an AI forecast without treating it as certain.

5. The Singularity Is Near: When Humans Transcend Biology (2005) - Ray Kurzweil - Viking - Kurzweil popularized the "law of accelerating returns," using log-scale charts of computing to argue that technology improves exponentially. A well-known example of trend extrapolation, useful for weighing both its insight and its risks.

6. [The 2025 AI Index Report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2025-ai-index-report) - Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI - An annual data report on AI benchmarks, adoption, investment, education, and policy. Documents the sharp drop in inference cost behind the chapter's discussion of falling AI costs.

7. [Can AI Scaling Continue Through 2030?](https://epoch.ai/blog/can-ai-scaling-continue-through-2030) - Epoch AI - Analyzes power, chip manufacturing, data scarcity, and latency as constraints on growth in training compute. A careful look at the limits to AI growth that could bend the doubling curve.

8. [DOE Releases New Report Evaluating Increase in Electricity Demand from Data Centers](https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-releases-new-report-evaluating-increase-electricity-demand-data-centers) - U.S. Department of Energy - Summarizes a Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory report on how much U.S. electricity data centers use now and may use by 2028. Grounds the chapter's discussion of AI energy use.

9. [Artificial Intelligence](https://ourworldindata.org/artificial-intelligence) - Our World in Data - Interactive charts of AI training compute and AI test scores relative to humans over time. Gives readers practice with long-run trend charts and logarithmic axes using a trusted, freely available data source.

10. [AI Competency Framework for Students](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-students) - UNESCO - Sets out 12 AI competencies across ethics, techniques, and system design for school curricula. Offers a starting point for the AI literacy the chapter recommends schools build while capability grows.
