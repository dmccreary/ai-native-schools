# Technology Adoption and the Diffusion of Innovation

## Summary

This chapter introduces the technology adoption curve: Rogers' diffusion of innovations, the five innovation attributes, adopter categories from innovators to laggards, the chasm between early adopters and the early majority, and the hype cycle. Readers finish able to place a new technology or practice on the adoption curve and explain why it might stall at the chasm.
