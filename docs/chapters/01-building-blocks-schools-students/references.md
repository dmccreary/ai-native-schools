# References: The Building Blocks of Schools, Students, and Learning

1. [Curriculum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curriculum) - Wikipedia - Surveys definitions of curriculum, from planned content to the full set of student experiences, and its history. Useful background for the chapter's distinction between what schools intend to teach and the learning outcomes they measure.

2. [Bloom's taxonomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloom%27s_taxonomy) - Wikipedia - Explains the original 1956 taxonomy and the 2001 revision, from remembering to creating. Gives readers the vocabulary of higher-order thinking that later chapters use to describe what mornings and afternoons should target.

3. [Testing effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Testing_effect) - Wikipedia - Summarizes research showing that recalling information strengthens memory more than rereading it, and its link to spaced practice. Grounds the chapter's retrieval practice section in decades of cognitive science.

4. Taxonomy of Educational Objectives, Handbook I: The Cognitive Domain (1956) - Benjamin S. Bloom (editor) - David McKay Company - The original source of the six-level hierarchy of thinking skills. Bloom's framework gave teachers a shared way to tell recall questions from analysis and creation tasks, which the chapter relies on.

5. Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning (2014) - Peter C. Brown, Henry L. Roediger III, and Mark A. McDaniel - Belknap Press of Harvard University Press - Credited with bringing retrieval practice, spacing, and interleaving to teachers in plain language, and with showing why learning that feels easy often fades fastest.

6. [Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) - What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences - A 2007 federal practice guide recommending spaced review and quizzing, each with an evidence rating. Shows school leaders how strongly the research supports the learning strategies this chapter introduces.

7. [The Learning Scientists: Downloadable Materials](https://www.learningscientists.org/downloadable-materials) - The Learning Scientists - Free posters, slides, and bookmarks for six study strategies, including spaced practice and retrieval practice. Practical classroom materials that turn the chapter's learning-science vocabulary into routines students can use.

8. [Retrieval Practice](https://www.retrievalpractice.org/) - Pooja K. Agarwal, RetrievalPractice.org - A cognitive scientist's site with over 100 free guides, classroom strategies, and research summaries on retrieval practice. Helps teachers see how low-stakes recall fits into an ordinary class period.

9. [Formative Assessment](https://www.edutopia.org/topic/formative-assessment) - Edutopia - A collection of articles and short videos on quick checks for understanding, questioning, and feedback during a lesson. Gives concrete examples of the formative assessment the chapter describes as checking learning while it happens.

10. [Fast Facts: Expenditures](https://nces.ed.gov/fastfacts/display.asp?id=66) - National Center for Education Statistics - Reports average U.S. public school spending per pupil and how it divides among salaries, benefits, and capital costs. Supplies the baseline numbers behind the chapter's section on school funding.
