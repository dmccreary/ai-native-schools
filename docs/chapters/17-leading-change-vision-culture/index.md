# Leading Change: Vision, Culture, and Early-Stage Concerns

## Summary

This chapter goes deeper into the early stages of leading change: Lewin's unfreezing, Kotter's guiding coalition and shared vision, Bridges' endings and losses, Schein's levels of culture, and the first stages of ADKAR and the Concerns-Based Adoption Model. Readers finish able to apply these early-stage tools to diagnose where a school is at the start of a change effort.
