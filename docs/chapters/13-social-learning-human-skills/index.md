# Social Learning and Human Skills

## Summary

This chapter introduces social constructionism and constructivism as the theoretical basis for the pro-social afternoon, along with the specific human skills it targets: collaboration, teamwork, communication, empathy, creativity, and social-emotional learning. Readers finish able to explain why students need practice working with other people even when AI can teach thinking skills well on its own.
