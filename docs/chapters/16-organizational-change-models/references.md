# References: Organizational Change Models

1. [Change management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change_management) - Wikipedia - Surveys the major change models, including Lewin's three steps, Kotter's eight steps, and Prosci's ADKAR. It gives readers one place to compare the models this chapter maps before later chapters apply them.

2. [Organizational culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organizational_culture) - Wikipedia - Defines organizational culture, including Schein's description of shared basic assumptions, and reviews how culture is studied and changed. It supports the chapter's section on organizational and school culture.

3. [Learning organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning_organization) - Wikipedia - Explains the learning organization concept and Senge's five disciplines: systems thinking, personal mastery, mental models, shared vision, and team learning. It previews ideas developed in Chapters 17 and 18.

4. Leading in a Culture of Change (Revised Edition) - Michael Fullan - Jossey-Bass - Fullan names the implementation dip, the drop in performance and confidence when people meet an innovation that needs new skills. Knowing the dip is normal keeps leaders from abandoning a reform too early.

5. The Fifth Discipline: The Art and Practice of the Learning Organization (Revised Edition) - Peter M. Senge - Doubleday/Currency - Senge's book introduced the five disciplines and argued that systems thinking ties the others together. It gives schools a model for continually learning rather than running one-time initiatives.

6. [CBAM: The Concerns-Based Adoption Model](https://www.air.org/resource/cbam-concerns-based-adoption-model) - American Institutes for Research - Overview of CBAM's three diagnostic tools: Innovation Configurations, Stages of Concern, and Levels of Use. It shows how leaders can match support to each teacher instead of relying on training alone.

7. [Michael Fullan](https://michaelfullan.ca/) - Michael Fullan - Fullan's site offers free handouts, articles, and a Professional Capital survey on educational change. It is a direct source for the educational-change thinking summarized in this chapter.

8. [What Makes a School a Learning Organisation?](https://ideas.repec.org/p/oec/eduaab/137-en.html) - OECD Education Working Paper 137 (Kools and Stoll) - Proposes seven action-oriented dimensions of a school as a learning organisation, from shared vision to learning-focused leadership. It translates Senge's business-oriented idea into school terms.

9. [How Principals Affect Students and Schools](https://wallacefoundation.org/report/how-principals-affect-students-and-schools-systematic-synthesis-two-decades-research) - Wallace Foundation - Synthesizes 219 studies on principal effects, including building a productive climate and promoting collaboration. It links school culture, a core idea in this chapter, to student and teacher outcomes.

10. [School Culture](https://www.edutopia.org/topic/school-culture) - Edutopia - A collection of practitioner articles and videos on belonging, teacher well-being, routines, and onboarding. It offers concrete examples of the visible behaviors and rituals that make up a school's culture.
