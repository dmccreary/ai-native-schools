# School Structures, Equity, and the Industrial-Era Legacy

## Summary

This chapter examines the structural and historical scaffolding of the school system: the bell schedule, the Carnegie unit, seat time, age-based grade levels, and the industrial-era school model, alongside achievement and equity gaps and alternatives like charter, virtual, and microschools. It also introduces how to judge evidence quality (independent evaluation versus self-reported results, preprints). Readers finish able to explain why schools are organized the way they are and what alternatives already exist.
