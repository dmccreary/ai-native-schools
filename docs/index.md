# AI-Native Schools

<figure markdown>
  [![AI-Native Schools: Mornings with AI, Afternoons with Each Other. Cover image of a student learning on a laptop in the morning light beside Indigo the barn owl, surrounded by students playing music, acting, building a robot, gardening and playing soccer in the afternoon](./img/cover.png){ width="100%" }](chapters/01-building-blocks-schools-students/index.md)
</figure>

Welcome to **AI-Native Schools**, a free, interactive intelligent textbook
about what schools could do with the time AI gives back. The book predicts
that within three to four years almost every student will have a free,
adaptive textbook. If that happens, the academic core can shrink to the
morning, and the afternoon can go to music, theater, sports, maker spaces,
and projects that students do with each other.

The book is honest that this change can go well or badly. Every story about
AI in schools shows both a benefit and a risk, and how a school can respond
to the risk.

## Who This Book Is For

K-12 teachers, principals and administrators, school board members,
education policymakers, curriculum and instructional designers, education
students, and parents. No programming or statistics background is needed.
See the [Course Description](course-description.md) for the full learning
objectives.
