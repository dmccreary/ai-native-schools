    Promise and Peril: Claims Audit¶

    Audit trail for every fact in data.json, following Phases 1–4 of the verified-infographic guide. Checked November 2026 with web search and page fetches. Notes paraphrase the source; they are not quotations.

    Status key: verified (two independent sources agree, or the primary paper plus one independent account), verified, one source (a single credible source; no second found), downgraded (kept only in softer words), removed (left off the poster).

    Claims¶

    Zone Claim Status Sources Supporting note
    alpha-benefit About two hours a day on adaptive software (2 Hour Learning); the rest on workshops and projects with guides verified S1, S2 Both describe the two-hour academic block and the guide-led rest of the day.
    alpha-benefit Began in Austin in 2014 verified S1, S2 S1 gives Austin, 2014; S2 says it started in Austin in 2014. One mirror names a different co-founder and a 2016 campus date, so the poster gives no founder names.
    alpha-risk About $40,000 a year at the original Austin campus verified S2, S1 S2 says the original Austin school charges $40,000; S1 says most campuses are about $40,000.
    alpha-risk About $65,000 a year at newer campuses such as Boston and New York verified S3, S4 S3: $65,000 at the Boston-area sites. S4: $65,000 at the Manhattan site. Miami reports differ by campus (about $50,000 in South Miami, $65,000 in Miami Beach), so Miami is not named on the poster. The poster never gives $65,000 as the price for every campus (AGENTS.md rule).
    alpha-risk Results claims rest on Alpha's own analysis, not independently reviewed verified S1, S2 S1: claims rest on internal analyses of MAP data, not independently reviewed. S2: results come from Alpha's own data, small samples.
    alpha-response No school response to the cost risk found verified (absence) S1, S2, S3 None of the three describes an affordability step or an independent evaluation. A lower-priced Texas campus is mentioned in S4 and S1's range, but no source calls it a response to cost, so the poster does not claim one. Band left empty.
    alpha-response The book's proposed response chapter Ch22 Taken from Chapter 22's framework table and labeled as the book's.
    unbound-benefit Tuition-free online charter in Arizona, grades 4–8, using 2 Hour Learning verified S5, S6, S1 S5: tuition-free virtual charter, grades 4–8. S6: online, grades 4–8. S1: links it to the 2 Hour Learning founders.
    unbound-benefit Approved 4–3 by the Arizona State Board for Charter Schools in December 2024 verified S7, S8 Both give 4–3. S7 dates the vote 16 Dec 2024; S6 (18 Dec 2024) says "Monday," which matches.
    unbound-risk About 10% proficient in math and 28% in ELA after 2025–26, against first-year targets of 60% and 65% verified, secondary S11, S12 Both report the figures from a slideshow made for the school's board. Neither is the primary state release, so the poster says "reportedly."

Presented as a claim, not a result. S10 quotes the head of school saying "twice the national rate," a different number, so the poster uses the website figure and calls it a claim.
    unbound-risk Pennsylvania denied a related application in Jan 2025; at least four states besides Arizona declined verified S9, S10, S1 S9: denial dated 29 Jan 2025. S10: at least four other states declined. S1 lists PA, NC, AR and UT.

    Downgraded or removed¶

    Claim Status Reason
    Unbound's "90 percent or more within two to three years" projection removed from poster Supported by S12 but not needed; the first-year targets (60%, 65%) make the same point.
    Alpha's price as 2.6 to 4.2 times public per-pupil spending of about $15,600 removed from poster Chapter's own calculation; not re-verified here and not needed.
    Founder names for Alpha School removed Sources disagree.
    Summit spin-off to an independent nonprofit (TLP Education) in 2019–20 removed Found in secondary summaries only; naming differs across sources.
    Wellington dropping Summit removed No source found that it did.
    Exact NYT withdrawal counts in McPherson removed Conflict between sources (see above).
    Unbound students "learning at twice the national rate" removed Head of school's spoken claim (S10) conflicts with the website's 2.8x claim.
